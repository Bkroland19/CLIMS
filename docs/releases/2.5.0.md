# CLIMS 2.5.0

- District focal persons create the accounts of the field workers in their own district (VHT, hotspot manager, delivery agent, and no other role) with `POST /users/district`, and list them with `GET /users/district`.
- Adds a sync log of what field phones send. The field app records each saved action and every attempt to send it, and uploads metadata only (`POST /sync-log`). MOH and administrators read it under **Audit → Sync log** (`GET /sync-log`, new `sync_logs:read` permission), and can match an entry to the server's audit log.
- Adds a geography tree with store and hotspot counts per region, district and subcounty (`GET /locations/geography`).
- Order and stockout lists filter by district, status and warehouse and search on the server, so MOH's national Orders lists page on the server instead of loading every order.
- Reports: a hotspot status report, field work not yet received from phones, and each report names its date basis. Receipts are counted by acknowledgement date.
- MOH lands on Analytics, with what needs watching on its Overview tab.
- Hotspots show their open notification and protect exact locations. National readers can no longer comment on hotspots.
- Signing out returns to a plain sign-in page, so the next user no longer lands on the previous user's page.

## Upgrade from 2.4.0

Back up PostgreSQL before deploying. The backend applies migrations 000040 and 000041 at startup:

- 000040 adds `users:district_read` and `users:district_create` and grants them to the admin and DCFP roles.
- 000041 creates the `mobile_sync_log` table and the `sync_logs:read` permission, granted to MOH and administrators.

There are no new production settings. Field phones need the 2.5.0 app to upload sync log entries.
